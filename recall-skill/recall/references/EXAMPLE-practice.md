# pre-mortem before committing a quarterly roadmap

## Signals
- A quarter's roadmap is about to be finalized with 3+ significant bets, OR stakeholders aligned suspiciously fast (sign of unexamined assumptions)
- NOT for small mid-quarter adjustments — overhead exceeds value

## Approach
1. Before sign-off, 30-min pre-mortem: "quarter's over and the roadmap failed — why?"
2. Each participant writes failure reasons silently (5 min), then share.
3. Cluster; for the top 3, add a mitigation to the plan or explicitly accept the risk.
4. Record accepted risks in the roadmap doc's "known risks" section.

## Success criteria
Roadmap doc has a "known risks" section with owners; at retro, no major failure traced to a risk raised in the pre-mortem but dropped silently.

## Pitfalls
- 2026-04: Skipping silent-writing (step 2) let the loudest voice anchor everyone — only 1 real risk surfaced. Keep step 2.
